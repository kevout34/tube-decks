How to mix
  1. Paste a YouTube link into each deck and press Load.
  2. Press Find BPM on each deck. The first time, it opens the audio engine window: choose this tab, switch on "Also share tab audio", then Share. The deck plays for about 8 seconds while it listens. No engine? Press Tap BPM in time with the beat instead, at least 4 times.
  3. Press Sync on the deck you want to follow the other one. The drift bar shows whether they're in time. Press Sync again to stop following.
  4. Repeat pads loop 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats, starting on the beat. Press the lit pad to let the track carry on.
  5. Spin a jog wheel while the track plays to nudge it ahead or behind. When paused, spinning scrubs through the track.

YouTube only changes speed in 5% steps, so a synced deck switches between the two closest steps to stay in time. Effects and Tune work on the whole mix. Some uploads can't be played on other sites. If one won't load, try another upload of the same song. BPM, beat position and hot cues are remembered for each video.
