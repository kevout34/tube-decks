How to mix

  1. Paste a YouTube link into each deck and press Load.
  2. Play a track and tap the Tap button on every beat, at least four times. That sets the BPM, and your last tap marks beat 1. You can also type the BPM in, then press Beat 1 on a downbeat.
  3. Choose a Master deck. On the other deck press Sync. It matches the speed, lines the beats up and turns Lock on so they stay together. The rail under the beat lights shows the drift: centred means in time.
  4. Loop pads lock to the beat grid. Press one to loop that many beats. Press another to change the length, or press the lit one or Exit to let the track run on.
  5. Hot cues: tap an empty pad to save a point, tap it again to jump there on the next beat. Right-click or shift-click a pad to clear it.

Limits from YouTube: speed moves in 0.05× steps between 0.25× and 2×, so Lock keeps synced decks in time by switching between the two nearest steps. Loops jump back with a seek, so very short loops can stutter a little. Some uploads block playback on other sites. If that happens, try a different upload of the same track. Your BPM, beat 1 and cues are saved per video in this browser.
